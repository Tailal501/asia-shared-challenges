Asia's Shared Challenges. Different Realities. Collective Opportunities.

Asia is a region of remarkable diversity, economic transformation and human potential — and home to some of the world's most persistent development inequalities, humanitarian crises and environmental challenges. This platform brings together development statistics, country comparisons, thematic evidence and opportunities for collective solutions across 18 Asian contexts, beginning with Pakistan.

Understanding Asia's development landscape

The Human Development Index ranges from 0.496 in Afghanistan and 0.544 in Pakistan to 0.937 in South Korea and 0.955 in Hong Kong SAR.

Pakistan records 7.9 expected years of schooling, compared with 16.6 in South Korea. Child mortality ranges from 56 per 1,000 live births in Pakistan to 2.8 in South Korea.

Female labour force participation stands at 5.1% in Afghanistan and 24.0% in Pakistan, compared with 68.6% in Vietnam and 77.4% in Cambodia.

These differences show why economic growth alone cannot describe social progress, inclusion, resilience or people's capacity to exercise their rights.
